Jaguars BrandStyle Guide

How to use the Del Sol Jaguars logos, colors, and type consistently across print, web, apparel, uniforms, presentations, and AI-generated materials.

Everyone creating Del Sol materials, including staff, coaches, clubs, ASB, and boosters, should check this page before designing anything new.

File formats

SVG is the vector master for the web, signage, and anything that will be scaled. PDF is vector too, keeps the Pantone spot colors, and is what most print shops and embroidery digitizers prefer. PNG files are 2400px on the long side with transparent backgrounds for slides, documents, and social posts. All versions downloads every color version of that mark in all three formats. Sport Logos

Every Del Sol team has four ready-made lockups pairing the roaring jaguar head with the sport name. Use these for team shirts, schedules, social posts, and banners instead of typing a sport name next to a logo. They are supplied in full color only.

Download all sport logos ↓
Which lockup?

Del Sol Stacked and Jaguars Stacked fit square-ish spaces like shirt fronts and social posts. Arched is the classic chest print and works on hoodies and team bags. Horizontal is for wide, short spaces: web headers, email signatures, banners, and the back of a cap.

03 Clear Space & Sizex = height of the DEL SOL letters

Clear Space

Keep the area around the logo free of text, photos, other logos, and the edge of the page so it can breathe and stay legible.

xxxx

Minimum clear space on every side equals x, the height of the letters in DEL SOL (the small top line of the Del Sol Jaguars word mark). For marks without lettering, such as the jaguar heads, sun marks, or Interlocking DS, use one quarter of the mark's width. When the logo is resized, the clear space scales with it.

04 Usage Rules4 do8 don't

Protect the Marks

Proportions, colors, and construction are fixed. If you find yourself dragging a corner handle, changing a fill color, or adding an effect, stop and grab a different file from the library instead.

✓ Do

Use the full-color logo on white or light backgrounds whenever color printing is available.

✓ Do

On Del Sol Orange, use the black & white version of an illustrated mark. Its white fills keep it visible.

✓ Do

Orange or gold lettering reads well on black for athletics and spirit graphics.

✓ Do

Use the Interlocking DS for small spaces: caps, sleeves, favicons, and app icons.

✕ Don't

Stretch, squash, or distort. Hold Shift when resizing.

✕ Don't

Recolor. Orange, gold, gray, black, and white only, as supplied.

✕ Don't

Add shadows, glows, outlines, bevels, or other effects.

✕ Don't

Rotate or tilt the logo.

✕ Don't

Put orange or gold lettering on orange or gold backgrounds. The letters have no outline and disappear.

05 ColorPantone · CMYK · RGB · HEXClick any value to copy

Color Palette

Del Sol Orange leads. Gold is the sunlight accent, Black anchors the outlines, and white keeps everything crisp. Gray tones appear only in the grayscale logo versions.

Del Sol Orange · shades & tints

Del Sol Gold · shades & tints

Base vs. tints

Base colors are for primary brand moments: logos, headers, uniforms, signage. Shades and tints are for supporting work such as web UI, text, charts, table fills, and backgrounds. For print, match the official Pantone or CMYK values.

Pantone

Pantone colors are official. Give print vendors the PMS number for spot-color jobs and the CMYK values for 4-color printing. On-screen colors may not match Pantone standards exactly; consult a current Pantone guide for color-critical work.

07 AccessibilityWCAG 2.24.5:1 body · 3:1 large text & UI

Contrast

WCAG requires at least 4.5:1 contrast for normal body text, and at least 3:1 for large text (18px and up, or 14px bold) and UI elements like icons, buttons, and input borders. Ratios below are calculated from the official HEX values.

ColorAs text on whiteAs text on blackWhite text on itBlack text on it
Never

White text on Del Sol Orange or Gold. White on orange measures about 2.9:1 and white on gold about 1.7:1, both under the 3:1 minimum. Put black text on orange or gold instead (7.2:1 and 12.1:1).

Avoid

Orange or Gold as text on white. Use Orange Shade 40% (#993E13) for headings and links on white; it passes AA. Keep bright orange and gold for accents, rules, and icons, and for text on black.

  • Orange Shade 40% on white passes AA for all text sizes. Use it for headings and links on web pages and documents.
  • Buttons in Del Sol Orange use black text, not white.
  • Orange on Gold, or Gold on Orange, is only about 1.7:1. Separate them with black or white.
  • Underline links regardless of color, so color isn't the only signal.

Open Sans

Paragraphs, captions, letters, forms, tables, and website body copy. Regular 400 and SemiBold 600, with Italic for light emphasis. Matches the district's body text.

Installing Del Sol Italic
  • Mac: double-click the .otf file, then click Install Font.
  • Windows: right-click the .otf or .ttf file and choose Install for all users.
  • Websites and Wix: upload the .woff2 file as a custom font.
  • Canva or Adobe Express: upload the .otf file as a brand font.
Spanish text

Del Sol Italic has no accent marks. Á, É, Ñ, Ü and the other accented letters print as plain A, E, N, U, and there is no ¿ or ¡. Set Spanish headlines in Open Sans, or check every word before printing so AÑO doesn't become ANO.

Important

Del Sol Italic does not replace the logo. Never type DEL SOL or JAGUARS in it as a stand-in for the word marks. It is a display face; don't set paragraphs in it. It also has no ™, ©, or ® symbols.

09 Name & Mascot

Writing the Name

✓ Do
  • Use Del Sol High School on first reference in formal writing, then Del Sol.
  • Write Del Sol as two words, each capitalized.
  • The team name is Jaguars: capital J, plural. One athlete or the mascot is a Jaguar.
  • Write Go Jaguars! or Del Sol Jaguars in spirit copy.
  • Include Oxnard Union High School District on official documents, letterhead, and forms.
✕ Don't
  • Write Delsol, DelSol, Del sol, or Del Soul. Spell-check won't catch them.
  • Use a lowercase "jaguars" or put the name in quotes.
  • Type the school or team name in place of a logo; place the word mark file instead.
10 AI & FlyersArtwork only · real logo · real fonts

AI Image Tools

Image generators can't reproduce the Del Sol jaguar exactly. Ask one to "include the Del Sol logo" and it invents a new, slightly wrong jaguar: different spots, missing step details, made-up lettering. Use AI for the artwork only, then add the real logo and text yourself.

  1. Generate artwork onlyBackgrounds, illustration, or photo-style scenes. No logos, mascots, text, or seals.
  2. Leave clear spaceAsk for empty, calm space in a corner or along one edge where the logo will go.
  3. Place the real logoDrop the official PNG or SVG into Canva, Adobe Express, Slides, or Word.
  4. Add text by handSet headlines in Del Sol Italic and body copy in Open Sans.
✕ AvoidCreate a flyer for Del Sol High School's homecoming with the Jaguars logo and school colors.

The tool will draw a fake jaguar and guess at "school colors."

✓ Use insteadGenerate a bright, photographic stadium-at-sunset background in warm orange #FF671F and golden #FFB81C light with deep black shadows. Leave empty negative space in the upper third. No text, no logos, no mascots, no animals.

Hex codes, not color names. Then add the logo and words yourself.

  • Never ask an AI tool to draw, recreate, or "improve" the jaguar, the sun marks, or the Interlocking DS.
  • Never upload the logo as a reference image for an AI tool to imitate.
  • Confirm clear space and contrast before you publish.
